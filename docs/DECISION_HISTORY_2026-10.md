  registered through the same CLI/MCP durable runner. Runner owns all reviewed

  absent destinations. Guidance publishes last through its existing action and
  pinned selection; a separate read-only confirmation binds the complete snapshot

  Repairs renew proposal approvals without resetting accounting; old placements
  and interrupted effects remain preserved. Setup grants no Git or pipeline-start
  authority. Reconstructed turns settle against their verified per-turn snapshots;
  owner loss during the second attempt produces a resumable pause without renewing
  recovery allowances. Managed guidance includes the exact finalization path.

  prerequisite publisher for an absent canonical-path-named sibling or an
  explicitly selected target-bound root. Only the selected infrastructure receives
  artifacts, finalization and configuration; target content, skills and Git controls
  remain protected inputs. Returning the exact configuration selector makes the
  prepared resources reusable without pointers or global registration. Guidance
  and terminal setup completion retain their later gates.

- **Integrated setup publication belongs to Runner.** The unregistered init
  foundation now prepares only reviewed integrated prerequisites under existing
  leases and journaled per-resource intents, identities and verified readback.
  Literal ignore additions preserve prior contents and negations; destination
  settings retain unrelated values. Existing finalization skills remain exact,
  with adjacent reviewed procedures adapting manual staging instructions to
  pipeline-owned effect boundaries. Guidance and public completion stay deferred,
  keeping partial setup distinct from verified usable infrastructure.
